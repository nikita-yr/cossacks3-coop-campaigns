[h1]Coop Campaigns[/h1]
Play the campaign missions of Cossacks 3 together with a friend. Both of you command the same nation: one army, one economy, two hetmans.

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Both players subscribe to the mod and enable it in the Mod Manager.
[*]Main menu > [b]Co-op[/b]. Log in to the GSC server: the co-op room is created automatically.
[*]Choose a campaign and a mission. Your friend joins the room from the lobby list and presses Ready.
[*]Press Start game. You can also start alone.
[/olist]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every campaign mission you own (original campaigns first, DLC campaigns if installed).
[*]All players control the campaign nation together.
[*]Mission scripts, objectives, allies and enemies work as in the single-player campaign.
[*]Co-op button in the main menu, translated into all game languages.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Beta. Saving during a multiplayer game is not supported yet.
[*]Difficulty is fixed to Normal (it must be the same on every PC).
[*]Both players need the same mods in the same order, and the DLC of the chosen campaign.
[*]Changes: historical battle room, main menu, in-game player list. Other mods that replace the same files may conflict.
[/list]

[b]Русский[/b]

Главное меню > «Кооператив», вход на сервер GSC: комната создаётся сама. Выберите кампанию и миссию, друг заходит в комнату из лобби и нажимает «Готов». Можно начать и одному. Бета: сохранение в сетевой игре пока не поддерживается, сложность всегда «Нормальная».
